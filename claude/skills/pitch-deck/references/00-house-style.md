# House style

The defaults, in one page. If you do not want to make decisions, copy what is here.

This file holds defaults and owns no numbers. Every size, count and length lives in the file that sources it:

- Type, grid, alignment, aspect ratio: `04-type-and-layout.md`
- Color and contrast: `05-color-and-access.md`
- Which deck, how long, how many pages: `02-artifact-and-length.md`
- The thresholds the audit enforces: `../SKILL.md`

Where this page and one of those files appear to disagree, the other file wins.

---

## Canvas and artifact

- 16:9 widescreen is the strong default. `04-type-and-layout.md` lists the named exceptions.
- Exact canvas: 33.867 by 19.05 cm, PowerPoint widescreen, landscape.
- Send a PDF. Keep the `.pptx` as the live and editable master, so you can present from it and edit it without a round trip through the exported file.
- One deck does not serve every purpose. `02-artifact-and-length.md` owns the deck system, the slide counts and the read-alone page count.

---

## Typography

- One sans-serif family is the default. Two is the ceiling.
- An optional monospace face for code or technical identifiers does not spend the second slot.
- Semibold headlines, regular body. That single weight contrast carries the hierarchy without extra colors or rules.
- Sizes: use the two tables in the Typography section of `04-type-and-layout.md`, one for a live deck and one for a read-alone deck. Do not carry sizes over from this page; it does not list any.
- Nothing on a slide goes below the size floor for its mode. The floors, and which one is sourced and which one is a judgment, are stated in `04-type-and-layout.md`.

---

## Color

- Background: white or off-white, or a dark neutral. Pick one and hold it across the deck.
- Main text: near-black on light, near-white on dark.
- One brand color plus one accent covers most decks.
- Semantic colors, meaning positive, negative and warning, are a named exception to the hue-family cap. They are not an extra family competing for a slot, and they are not license to add decoration.
- The cap itself, how tints are counted, and the contrast targets live in `05-color-and-access.md`.

## Layout

- A consistent grid, reused on every slide. `04-type-and-layout.md` gives the working grid.
- Generous white space. Strong left alignment.
- One main visual zone per slide: a single chart, image, diagram or screenshot that the eye lands on. Two competing visual zones is the most common way a clean slide turns busy.
- A consistent footer and source treatment on every slide that needs one.
- No visual clutter: no drop shadows, no stray rules, no decorative shapes carrying no information.

### Source lines

Keep the source line on the slide, set at the size floor for the mode. Only overflow provenance, meaning the full citation, method notes and caveats, moves to the appendix.

The one case for dropping the on-slide source line is a live projected deck, where the presenter says the provenance out loud. Do not strip source lines from a deck that will be read alone.

---

## Content

- One conclusion per slide.
- Assertion-style titles: the title states the claim, not the topic.
- Evidence sits immediately under the claim it supports.
- Show the product visually rather than describing it.
- Bottom-up market sizing.
- Traction shown as a trend, not a single flattering number.
- A clear go-to-market, a clear business model, and honest competition.
- A milestone-based fundraising ask: what the money buys, not just how much.
- A large appendix, so the main sequence can stay short.

### Where the explanation goes

Split by mode. In a live deck, the explanation belongs in the speaker notes. In a read-alone deck it belongs in the appendix, because an emailed PDF carries no narration. Do not move detail into speaker notes for a deck nobody will present.

## Delivery

- A 30-second company explanation you can say from memory, with no slides.
- Core story length: see `02-artifact-and-length.md`.
- A longer version prepared, for the meeting that runs long or goes deep.
- Interruptions practiced, so a question in the middle does not derail the sequence.
- Key numbers memorized.
- An appendix you can search and jump into while talking. `07-delivery.md` covers the rehearsal detail.
